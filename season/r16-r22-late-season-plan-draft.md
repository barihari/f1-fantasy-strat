## Objective for R16-R22

1. Convert structural advantage (post-Wildcard Ferrari + Mercedes constructors) into weekly consistency.
2. Maximize weekly 2X boost placement with minimal transfer churn.
3. Protect against calendar risk at R21-R22 (Qatar/Abu Dhabi): do not rely on those rounds for chip plans.

---

## Chip State Branches at R16

### Branch A — No Negative already used (e.g., Canada rain)
- No chips left from R16 onward.
- Focus becomes pure transfer discipline + boost optimization.

### Branch B — No Negative still available
- Hold until a true chaos trigger:
  - Street circuit + unstable weather
  - High incident probability
  - Practice data showing fragile reliability
- Best remaining targets: **R16 Singapore** or **R19 Brazil**.
- Do not hold it to R21/R22 because of geopolitical risk.

## Transfer Rules for the Final 7 Rounds

1. Default to **0-1 transfer** per race.
2. Never take a -10 unless protecting a known 25-40 point swing.
3. Use a **2-race validation rule**:
   - One bad race: hold.
   - Two bad races + poor practice pace: move.
4. Prioritize constructor stability; most late points are lost by breaking strong constructor pairings.

---

## Boost Assignment Logic (R16-R22)

- **Low-overtake/track-position races (R16 Singapore):** boost best qualifier.
- **High-overtake races (R17 Austin, R18 Mexico, R19 Brazil):** boost highest position-gain ceiling.
- **Uncertain rounds (R21-R22):** boost safest premium with strongest baseline points floor.

## Geopolitical Risk Protocol (R21-R22)

Weekly check from R19 onward:
1. Confirm race status on official calendar.
2. If cancellation/relocation risk increases, avoid aggressive restructuring for R21-R22.
3. If either race is replaced by an unknown venue, use conservative lineup construction (no speculative punts).

## Win Conditions (R16-R22)

Primary:
- Maintain constructor edge and avoid penalty transfers.

Secondary:
- Hit correct 2X in at least 5 of final 7 rounds.
- Keep one flexible transfer entering each race lock where possible.
- If No Negative remains, deploy it before R21.

Failure to avoid:
- Saving chips or major transfer plans for Qatar/Abu Dhabi and losing deployment value if calendar changes.
